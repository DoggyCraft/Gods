1.0.0
-------------------------------
Requirements
* Minecraft 1.21.3 or newer, including 26.x (Spigot or Paper), and Java 21 or newer. Servers on older versions can keep using 0.9.x.
* Vault is no longer used. Permissions go through Bukkit (LuckPerms and the like work as usual).

Upgrading from 0.9.x (back up your plugins/Gods folder first)
* config.yml is upgraded to a new, commented layout with clearer key names; the old file is kept as config.yml.v1.bak. Gods no longer rewrites config.yml on every start.
* Language files are converted to MiniMessage in plugins/Gods/lang/<language>/ (messages.yml and voices/); your changes are kept and the old files are moved to lang/old/.
* gods.yml and holyland.yml get a data version; each is backed up (.v1.bak) before it is upgraded. Priests stored by name become UUIDs.
* Holy lands from 0.9.x can't be kept: they were stored under a broken key that mixed up regions and worlds. They are backed up and dropped; gods claim land again as believers build and pray at altars. Neutral lands must be set again with /g setsafe.
* Quests of types that no longer exist are removed.
* The descent (npc.descend in config.yml from earlier 1.0.0 builds) became the descended rank: config.yml is upgraded to config-version 5, npc.descend moves to ranks.descended (ascend-grace-hours to ranks.drop-grace-hours), and the old file is kept as config.yml.v4.bak.
* Existing gods get a rank once, on their first turn after the upgrade: the highest rank their believers, power, priest, mood, altars, holy land and age allow, with their lifetime stats (prayers, sacrifices, quests, converts, which Gods didn't count before) set to what that rank asks for. A god that had descended stays descended.
* Altar blocks moved into the god themes. Lists in config.yml's altars.block-types that were still the old defaults are removed, so those god types get their new themed blocks (the old block still works); lists you changed are kept and replace the theme's blocks for that god type. Existing altars and their signs keep working.

New
* Themed god types: each god type has its own holy and unholy mobs and foods, sacrifice needs, slay quest targets, curse mobs (evil curses with wither skeletons and vexes, frost with strays, the sea with drowned, ...), peaceful mobs its holy land lets spawn when the god allows no mob spawning (besides the old ones: the sea's dolphins and fish, the sun's camels, ...), altar blocks and altar sign style, with mobs, foods and blocks up to 26.x (bees, foxes, axolotls, frogs, camels, sniffers, armadillos, breezes, allays, wardens, creaking, happy ghasts, berries, honey, stews, ...). The themes are built into the jar; plugins/Gods/themes.yml holds only your changes (a list replaces Gods' list, add: and remove: change it) and is never rewritten. Names your server's Minecraft version doesn't have are skipped.
* New altar blocks for every god type (prismarine and sea lanterns for the sea, packed and blue ice for frost, every copper block for thunder, amethyst and chiseled bookshelves for wisdom, crying obsidian and sculk for evil, moss and azalea for nature, cherry wood and pink blocks for love, end stone bricks and purpur for the moon, ...), besides the old ones.
* Any torch, soul torch, redstone torch, lantern, soul lantern or candle (lit or not), and copper torches and lanterns where the server has them, lights an altar.
* Write God, Goddess or Deity on another line of a new altar sign to choose the god's gender; without it the god chooses and says so. The light no longer decides the gender.
* Non-binary gods: a Deity ("Deity of the Sea") speaks with both the male and the female voice of its type, picked anew for each thing it says. What gods say about themselves fits every gender: "I am the only true <gender>" and the holy book name the god's own gender, and the holy book no longer calls every god "he". Voice lines know <gender>.
* Priests can change their god's gender with /g gender <god|goddess|deity> (permission gods.priest.gender), once per priests.gender-change-cooldown-hours (24 by default); the god tells its believers. Plugins can do it too (GodsApi.setGender, GodGenderChangeEvent).
* Altar signs are tagged and written in their god type's style (text, colors, sign dye and glowing text; altar.sign.* in messages.yml). Old altar signs are still recognized by their text and restyled when first used.
* Pray by right-clicking the altar block or its light, not only the sign. Sneaking, items that work on the block (flint and steel on a candle, axes, honeycomb, ...) and blocks with a use of their own (note blocks, jukeboxes, candle cakes, ...) keep their vanilla click.
* Themed effects: particles and a sound rise around the altar when you pray (brighter when the god is pleased, darker when it is angry, with the god type's symbol floating up), a burning sacrifice floats up, spins and fades with a sign of whether the god wanted it, and blessed and cursed players get a glow and chime or a dark swirl. Each god type's look is the effects section of its theme; config.yml's effects section switches them (all or each kind) and sets how close players must be. Only nearby players see them; the floating items are never saved.
* With GriefPrevention installed, new altars can only be built in a claim where the builder has build trust (altars.require-claim, on by default); existing altars keep working. Without GriefPrevention nothing changes.
* The god's name and title float above its praying altars, in the style of its god type (altars.floating-text: all, main or off; altars.floating-text-view-distance-blocks). The texts are never saved with the world.
* Every god has a main altar: the altar it was founded at, or for gods from before, their oldest altar as Gods finds it. It gets its own floating line and /g info shows believers where it is. Priests can choose another with /g setaltar (also /g sethome; permission gods.priest.setaltar), looking at the altar, and the god tells its believers. When the main altar is destroyed, the god's oldest remaining altar takes its place and the god tells its priests. Plugins see it in GodInfo.mainAltar().
* Gods have ranks, like forum ranks: spirit, recognized, descended, exalted and greater, each named in the god type's style (themes.yml: the sea's Tide, Current, Breaker, Maelstrom and Leviathan; the default Spirit to Greater God). A god rises one rank at a time on its turn when it meets every condition of the next (config.yml's ranks: believers, power, a priest, its mood, a main altar, time with a priest or at its rank, and what its religion has done in its whole life: prayers, accepted sacrifices, completed quests, altars, holy land and converts). Its mood and its religion's activity never make it fall (an angry god just stops rising); losing its priest does at once, and too few believers after a grace period.
* Ranks unlock things (ranks.unlocks): the holy book and quests at recognized, holy artifacts and new alliances and wars at exalted, a second priest and an aura (Regeneration I) for believers in their god's holy land at greater, and more holy land with every rank. Commands of a locked feature say which rank it needs.
* Rising to a rank is a moment: an effect at the main altar (grander for higher ranks), the god tells its believers, and a greater god is announced to the whole server. Altar effects grow with the rank: a spirit's prayers flicker, a greater god's are the grandest.
* /g rank [god] shows a god's rank and, to its believers, what its next rank needs, condition by condition; /g info, /g list, the floating text above main altars and PlaceholderAPI (%gods_rank%) show the rank.
* Plugins see ranks through the API: GodInfo.rank(), GodsApi.getRankProgress and setRank, GodRankChangeEvent (a rise can be cancelled).
* A god descends to its altar when it rises to the descended rank: once its religion has a main altar, a priest for a day, three believers and a god in at least a neutral mood (ranks.descended in config.yml), its god appears in front of the main altar as a figure, with lightning (harmless) and its effects, and tells its believers; right-clicking the figure prays like the altar does, and the figure turns to players nearby. It ascends again when it falls below that rank: without a priest, or after a day with too few believers. The figure is a vanilla mannequin on Minecraft 1.21.9 and newer, or made by Citizens or FancyNpcs when one is installed (npc.provider: auto, builtin, citizens, fancynpcs or off); it is never saved with the world or in their files.
* God figures wear a skin for their gender from their theme (npc.skin in themes.yml: god, goddess and deity, each a signed texture, e.g. from mineskin.org; player names are not taken). Every god has one: the default theme has a god, a goddess and a deity, every type has its own god and all but party (Dionysus) their own goddess (thunder's Zeus and a storm goddess, the sun's Amaterasu, wisdom's Athena, …), and the moon its own deity; a deity wears its type's deity skin, else its type's god skin, else the default ones. When a god's gender changes, its figure changes skin. A priest can give the figure their own current skin with /g godskin, or a player's with /g godskin <player> (permission gods.priest.godskin; /g godskin reset gives back the theme's). Plugins get the figure with GodsApi.getNpc and isGodNpc, the descent with GodInfo.descended() and GodDescendEvent / GodAscendEvent.
* Gods keeps track of where altars are (plugins/Gods/altars.yml): new altars are added when built, existing ones as their chunks load or when they are used, and destroyed ones are dropped.
* Mobs and items without a name in messages.yml show a readable one (Cooked Beef) instead of COOKED_BEEF.
* Quests work again (pilgrimage, slay, item sacrifice and give-items quests); they were never generated before.
* Holy artifacts work: every god type has artifacts, each power does what its lore says, and powers have a cooldown. New artifacts are as strong as the god's rank when it gives them (1 up to recognized, as before, then 2, 3 and 4 for descended, exalted and greater gods; fireballs and ice spikes stay as they are), and their lore shows the real reach of freeze, nature and teleport and how hard an earthquake hits. Existing artifacts keep their strength.
* An API for other plugins (gods-api on JitPack): read gods and believers, make gods speak (custom text or their own voice lines), act as a god (bless, curse, heal, give items and artifacts, smite, quests, priests, war and alliance), and events for prayers, believers, priests, blessings, quests, holy land and relations. GodSpeakEvent lets e.g. an NPC plugin say a god's words.
* All messages are MiniMessage and can be changed, including the ones that were hard-coded.
* /g reload reloads everything, and reads only the data files you edited on disk.
* Data files are saved in the background and never overwritten when they can't be read (a copy is kept as <file>.broken-<time>).
* Gods never deletes gods, priests, believers or holy lands because another data file couldn't be read, and never saves over a data file you edited on disk (run /g reload to use your edit). Every data file is backed up (.v1.bak) before 1.0.0 first rewrites it.
* general.worlds: [] means every world, also worlds loaded later by other plugins.
* Anonymous statistics on bStats.

Fixed (a selection)
* Blessing or cursing a player could make gods.yml unloadable and wipe every god.
* Abandoned holy land cleanup deleted every holy land.
* Wars and alliances were never recognized; attacks on holy land could not be started or ended.
* Praying at an altar sign opened the sign editor and destroyed the altar on 1.20+; any wood type of sign now works.
* Arrows, TNT and explosions got through holy land protection; neutral land didn't stop mob spawns; defenders couldn't fight back.
* Titles, holy books, religion chat (mutes, % in god names) and many commands work again.
* Settings that were saved but never read (bibles on join, give-items quests, item blessing power, ...) now work. A god's own PvP and mob damage settings now apply even when the defaults are on. priests.assign-command / remove-command now run.

Changed
* A religion's home is its main altar: /g home takes believers to the front of it, facing the altar, or onto the nearest safe spot if that one is blocked (never into lava, water, fire or the void). /g sethome is the same as /g setaltar, for priests only, so priests.only-priests-can-set-home is removed (config.yml becomes version 4; the old file is kept as config.yml.v3.bak) and so is the gods.sethome permission. A god without a main altar keeps the home it had. GodInfo.home() is the spot in front of the main altar.
* gods.setsafe is for ops only.
* effects.prayer: false now means no effect at prayers at all; the old puff of flames no longer plays instead.
* Holy artifact powers can no longer grief or kill: teleport only lands on a safe spot, earthquake only hurts the creatures it hits, ice spikes melt back, tame only takes wild animals, and the gods' lightning no longer starts fires or changes creatures.
* Holy land only keeps out mobs the world spawns itself, not spawn eggs, breeding or golems.
* /g yes and /g no work for players without a god (to accept an invitation).
* Holy laws, prophecies, instruction books, global quests, holy war and slay dragon quests (never finished) and /g answer are removed.
